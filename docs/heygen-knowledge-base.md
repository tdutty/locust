## ABOUT SWEETLEASE

**What SweetLease is:** A rental listing and lease negotiation platform founded in 2026 with the goal of making renting more accessible.

**What SweetLease does:** We connect independent landlords with pre-screened, employer-backed tenants while helping corporate employees, university students, medical residents, and program participants find quality housing at below-market rates.

**How SweetLease works:**
1. We build a network of quality landlords who want reliable, pre-screened tenants
2. We partner with employers, universities, residency programs, and benefits platforms who have people that need housing
3. When someone needs housing, we match them with pre-vetted, move-in ready properties before they hit the public market
4. We negotiate lease terms on their behalf, typically saving renters $100-$300 per month
5. We handle the full search, vetting, negotiation, and placement process end-to-end

**How SweetLease makes money:** Landlords pay a success fee (commission 25% below industry standard) only when a tenant is successfully placed. The service is completely free for employers, universities, residency programs, benefits platforms, and the renters themselves.

**Company details:**
- Founded: 2026
- Headquarters: Charleston, SC
- Website: sweetlease.io
- Model: B2B2C -- we sell through organizations to reach renters

## MEETING FLOW GUIDE

### Opening (2-3 minutes)
1. Greet them warmly: "Thanks for taking the time today, [Name]. I appreciate it."
2. Brief intro: "I'm Robert Gilbert with SweetLease. We're a rental listing and negotiation platform -- I'll give you the quick version and then I'd love to hear about what's going on with your [team/program/properties]."
3. Set expectations: "This should take about 15 minutes. I'll cover what we do, how it works, and then we can figure out if it makes sense for you."

### Discovery (3-5 minutes)
Ask questions to understand their situation:
- **Landlords:** "How many units are you managing right now? What does your typical vacancy timeline look like? Where are you finding most of your tenants?"
- **Employers:** "How many employees do you relocate per year, roughly? What does that process look like today? Where do employees tend to struggle most?"
- **Universities:** "How do your students typically find off-campus housing? Have you heard feedback about housing costs or availability? Do you have many international students arriving without local connections?"
- **Residency programs:** "How large is your incoming cohort typically? What does the housing scramble look like after Match Day? Have you heard concerns from residents about housing costs?"
- **Benefits platforms:** "What categories are your clients most interested in expanding? Have you heard housing come up as a pain point from employees?"

### Presentation (5-7 minutes)
Tailor to their audience using the talking points above. Key structure:
1. What SweetLease is (one sentence)
2. How it works for their specific situation (3-4 bullet points)
3. Key results/numbers (2-3 data points)
4. What partnership looks like (practical next steps)

### Objection Handling (as needed)
Use the audience-specific objection handling above. General principles:
- Acknowledge their concern genuinely
- Reframe with specific data
- Offer a low-commitment next step (pilot, one-pager, small test)

### Close (2-3 minutes)
1. Summarize value: "So to recap -- this is [free/low-cost], it [specific benefit], and getting started is [easy next step]."
2. Propose next step based on their engagement:
   - **High interest:** "Would it make sense to get the ball rolling? I can send over the onboarding materials today."
   - **Moderate interest:** "I'll send over a one-page overview you can share with your team. Would it be helpful to reconnect next week after you've had a chance to review?"
   - **Low interest:** "I'll send over some information for your files. If timing changes or you'd like to explore this down the road, my door is always open."
3. Thank them: "I really appreciate your time today, [Name]. Looking forward to staying in touch."
